## 3. Not a clone

A5K is **not a clone**. It contains no Commodore code, ships no Kickstart ROM and
offers no binary compatibility with Amiga software. It is an independent
re-issue of the architecture's ideas — and those ideas are free to use: the
relevant patents expired by 2012, and the US semiconductor mask-work term is
ten years.

What remains protected, and is respected here, is Commodore's and its
successors' *code* (the Kickstart ROMs), the *trademarks*, and the original
*documentation*. None of it is reproduced.
